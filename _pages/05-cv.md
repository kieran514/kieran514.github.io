Research Experience
My doctoral research focuses on developing depth estimation systems that are robust and reliable across diverse and challenging conditions (e.g., adverse weather, low light). My work eliminates the need for expensive ground truth data by using self-supervised learning, with a focus on two key areas:

Weather & Lighting Robustness: Developed Robust-Depth, a method using augmentation-based pseudo-supervision to force models to learn consistent depth estimates regardless of visual conditions.

Effective Baseline Boosting: Created BaseBoostDepth, a novel training strategy that allows networks to effectively learn from larger time separations in video, harnessing stronger geometric cues to achieve state-of-the-art results.
