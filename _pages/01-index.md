Welcome! I'm a Computer Vision PhD student at Aston University, diving deep into the world of Self-Supervised Monocular Depth Estimation. My passion lies in teaching machines to perceive depth and reconstruct the 3D world from simple 2D images. I focus on creating robust algorithms that can 'see' clearly, even when the weather turns foul or the lighting fades, pushing the boundaries of applications like autonomous driving, immersive VR/AR, and underwater robotics.
