## Bridging Pixels and Perception

Welcome! I'm Kieran Saunders, a Computer Vision PhD student at <span class="blue">Aston University</span> diving deep into the world of **Self-Supervised Monocular Depth Estimation**. My passion lies in teaching machines to perceive depth and reconstruct the 3D world from simple 2D images – turning flat pixels into rich spatial understanding.

My journey started with a BSc in Mathematics, building a strong foundation for tackling complex visual problems. Now, I focus on creating robust algorithms that can 'see' clearly, even when the weather turns foul or the lighting fades, pushing the boundaries of applications like **autonomous driving**, **immersive VR/AR**, and **underwater robotics**.

I've had the privilege of sharing my work at top-tier conferences like **ICCV** and **BMVC**, translating challenging research into tangible progress. Explore my site to learn more about my projects and publications!
