My Mission

The next generation of AR and robotics depends on systems that work reliably in the wild, not just in the lab. My research in self-supervised learning is dedicated to solving this challenge. I create models that learn from vast amounts of unlabeled data, making them inherently more robust and scalable—ready for the complexities of real-world application.
